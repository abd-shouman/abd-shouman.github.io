Job agent privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who uses it

Job agent is a personal tool used only by its owner, Abd Shouman, with his own Google account. It has no other users.

What it accesses

It asks for one Google permission: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It searches only for job-related emails, such as replies from companies he applied to, applicant-tracking systems and job sites, and opens only those.

How the data is used

  • Each opened email is read to work out what it says about an application (for example "received", "interview" or "rejected"). The result is saved in a local database on the owner's computer.
  • To make that reading, the text of a job-related email may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the reading. Nothing else is shared with anyone.
  • The email's full text is not stored; a log of each email opened (sender, subject, date, why it was opened) stays on the owner's computer.
  • The tool never sends, deletes, archives or labels email, and never uses the data for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Job agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the tool's settings page, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The local data can be deleted from the owner's computer.
